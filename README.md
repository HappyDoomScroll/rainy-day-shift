# The Rainy Day Shift

A functional, static browser prototype for practicing supportive leadership through a fictional rainy-day shift at Silverline Park.

[Try the live prototype](https://HappyDoomScroll.github.io/rainy-day-shift/)

## Run locally

Download the repository and open `index.html` in a modern browser. Alternatively, run `python3 -m http.server` from the repository folder and visit `http://localhost:8000`.

Keep `index.html`, `app.js`, `story.js`, `style.css`, `maya.png`, and `queue.png` together. The core experience needs no installation, account, backend, or internet connection. Optional speech-to-text may require internet access.

## What learners practice

Learner instructions come before the story. Learners choose a practice focus, then:

- Separate what they noticed from what they inferred, consider a possible support need, and use a guiding question to check it.
- Type or rehearse a response before exploring an authored conversational approach.
- Follow the consequences, practice a follow-up, and choose a specific action to carry into their next shift.

Three openings and two follow-ups create six endings. There is no overall score or preferred support hypothesis. Characters respond to the selected authored approach; personal wording is for rehearsal and is not automatically assessed.

## Speaking and privacy

Aloud rehearsal uses no microphone. Optional speech-to-text asks for permission and may send audio to the browser provider for processing. Compatibility varies by browser and device. Typing remains available, and transcripts are editable.

The app does not save audio. Writing stays in the open page unless the learner downloads their notes; reloading clears it. There is no analytics service or server-side response collection.
