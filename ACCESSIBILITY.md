# Accessibility review

Accessibility work is partial. This prototype is not certified or claimed to conform fully to WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Implemented

Native buttons and form controls; labeled and grouped choices; visible keyboard focus; heading focus after scene changes; dialogs with Escape dismissal and focus restoration; narrative and character-expression text equivalents; reduced-motion support; optional sound; no time limits; and no required dragging, speaking, or microphone use.

## Checked in the available browser

All six story paths and debriefs, keyboard choice controls, Enter activation, dialog focus return, 320-pixel reflow, writing persistence within a playthrough, replay, note download, and the speech disclosure before microphone access. Flat text and control colors were reviewed and low-contrast labels and borders corrected.

## Still to verify

A complete WCAG 2.2 AA audit; end-to-end VoiceOver and NVDA use; actual 200% and 400% zoom; text-spacing overrides; Windows forced-color rendering; and real microphone transcription on each target browser and device. Representative learner testing is also pending.

Typing and microphone-free rehearsal remain available when browser speech recognition is unsupported or fails.
